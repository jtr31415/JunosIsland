## What it was before

`main.ts` `grownUps()` → menu item `wipe` → one `askConfirm` → `store.removeProfile(PROFILE)`
→ `location.reload()`. So it *did* already ask — one yes/no — but it was
all-or-nothing: `removeProfile` deletes the whole profile document, taking her
island, her animals, her ladder, her stage stats and her name together. A parent
who wanted her maths to start over had to destroy her animals to get it.

## What it is now

The menu item now reads **"Start again — choose what to clear"** and opens a
tick screen with three independent boxes, then a confirm that reads back only
what is going.

| Box | Label on screen | Clears |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Her island and her animals** | `tiles` (back to `createFlow()`'s one grass hex at 0,0), `pets`, `bankedTiles`, `plot`, `readProgress`, `sumProgress`, `tilesEarned`, `honeymoonTiles`, `pay` (re-stamped to today's `itemPay()`), `openingSeen` → false, `openCollections`/`lastOpened` → `NOTHING_OPENED` |
| 2 | **What she is working on** | `attainment` → `createAttainment()`, `onceFlags` → `[]` |
| 3 | **Her name** | `childName` → `''` |

**Never cleared by any box, deliberately:** `calmColours` (a colour-comfort
accessibility setting a grown-up chose, which none of the three questions
mentioned) and `persistGranted` (the browser's answer about storage, not hers —
re-asking would be a worse experience for no gain). This is a small change from
the old behaviour, where `removeProfile` took them too.

### Box 2 covers the whole harness record

Everything academic lives inside one object, `Attainment`
(`src/island/harness.ts:186`), so replacing it wholesale cannot leave a rung
behind. What that one line clears, enumerated:

- `PathAttainment.mode` (`auto | manual | hold`) → back to `auto`
- `PathAttainment.honeymoonFrom` → null
- `PathAttainment.offer` — `lastOfferDay`, `declinedDay`, `daysSinceDecline`, `lastCountedDay`
- `StageStats` per rung — `ticked` (**the ladder position**: there is no stored
  level number; the rung IS which stages are ticked against the ordered
  `STAGES` table), `attempts`, `ewma`, `latencies` (ring of 30), `early`
  (first 10, frozen), `sessions` (last 6 days), `rescues` (last 10), `probes`
  (last 12)

Three things that look like academic state and are **not** stored, so nothing
needs clearing: the weakness lean (derived each draw from `ewma`/`attempts`),
the mercy counter and `wrongRun` (closure-local in `createHarness`, die with the
tab), and the settled/whisper-retired set (derived). A wipe reloads the page, so
the session-local ones go anyway.

### >>> WHERE THE INTRO ASSESSMENT PLUGS IN

Joe's forward reference — *"once available this will reset the intro assessment
stage that we have not built yet"* — is **not built and not stubbed**. Nothing
was invented for it.

When it is built, its "has been sat / what it concluded" record belongs
**inside `Attainment`**, in which case `wipeSave`'s `out.attainment =
createAttainment()` already clears it and this card needs no further work. If it
genuinely cannot live there, clear it **on the line beside that one and nowhere
else** — there is a marked comment block at that exact spot in
`src/island/save.ts` (search `>>> WHERE THE INTRO ASSESSMENT PLUGS IN`).

## The three hard calls, and why

**1. The economy goes with the island, not with the academic box.**
`readProgress`, `sumProgress`, `tilesEarned`, `honeymoonTiles` and
`bankedTiles` are earned work toward the next egg and the next tile, not a
measurement of how she is doing. Brief §19 says earned work is hers, and "wipe
academic progress" is a request to re-measure her, not to charge her again. A
fresh island naturally starts with a fresh purse, so they go there.

*Consequence worth knowing:* `readProgress` does double duty — it is both the
currency toward the next egg and the reading book's page cursor
(`pagesRead(readProgress)`, `balance/index.ts:558`). So an island wipe also
rewinds the book to page one. That is coherent (fresh island, fresh book) but it
is a coupling, and it is the reason `readProgress` could not simply be filed
under "academic".

**2. The academic box clears `honeymoonFrom` but NOT `honeymoonTiles`.** They
are not two halves of one switch. `honeymoonFrom` (inside `Attainment`) is a
live discount that ends when the measurement it was granted against is thrown
away. `honeymoonTiles` (in the flow) is a **price offset for tiles she already
bought** — `sumsForTile` computes `tileCostPast(tilesEarned + 1 -
honeymoonTiles)`. Zeroing it would silently make her next tile dearer. That is
the same half-wipe this card exists to prevent, pointing the other way. Pinned
by a test.

**3. `onceFlags` go with the academic box.** They are the once-only teaching
moments (INTRO-TEN and its successors), keyed to where she is on the ladder.
Reset the ladder and leave them, and she re-climbs to the rung that introduces
tens carrying a save that says the game already showed her. `save.ts:108-124`
files them as "a sibling of attainment, not a measurement" — that is about which
*type* they belong in, not about which wipe they follow.

## THE LANDMINE — do not "simplify" the island wipe to `tiles: []`

`fromSave` (`src/island/save.ts:278`) treats an empty tile array as *"no save at
all"* and its fresh branch returns `childName: ''` and `persistGranted: null`.
So an island wipe written the obvious way — empty the tiles — **silently
destroys her name without anybody ticking the name box**, which is exactly the
coupling the three boxes exist to break. It is also invisible to any test that
inspects the JSON blob rather than loading it.

A fresh island is therefore written as `createFlow()`'s **one grass tile at
(0,0)**, never as `[]`. There is a comment on that line, a comment on
`wipeSave`, and a test named *"DOES NOT TAKE THEIR NAME — the trap this whole card
exists to break"*. Reverting the line to `[]` reddens three tests. Verified.

`fromSave`'s fresh branch was left alone deliberately (scope), but it is the
thing to be careful of if anyone touches this seam again.

## Guard rails on the destructive action (brief §19)

- **Every box starts unticked.** A screen that opened with anything pre-ticked
  would let one stray thumb take her animals.
- **The red button is `disabled` and reads "nothing ticked"** until a box is
  ticked, then wakes to "wipe what is ticked".
- **Each row says what it takes in counts** — "3 friends and 12 pieces of land",
  not "island data" — **and what it leaves**. A parent cannot infer independence
  from the layout.
- **The confirm reads back only the ticked rows**, line by line, then "It cannot
  be undone."
- Full journey: gear → DDMM PIN → menu → tick → red button → yes.

`src/island/species/name-pins.json` was **not touched**, and wiping animals does
not release pinned names — the pins are canonical data for every child, not her
save.

### Why `put`, not `removeProfile`

`wipeIsland` reads the blob, computes the wiped one, and `put`s it — the same
call every other change to her island already uses, so it lands in both
localStorage and IndexedDB and in the ring, and the newest thing any recovery
path can find is the wiped save. `removeProfile` is used only when there is no
save on disk at all. There is no special case for "all three ticked": a special
case would reintroduce exactly the coupling the card removes.

## Verified in a real browser

`npm run dev`, Chromium, real durable store (IndexedDB + localStorage), not
jsdom. Named the child Juno, then:

1. **Academic-only wipe** → page reloaded with **no name prompt** and the island
   intact. Re-opening the wipe screen showed *"She is called Juno."* — so the
   name and the island survived a full round trip through IndexedDB.
2. **Island-only wipe** → single grass hex, the opening story replaying, and the
   signpost and document title still reading **"Juno's Island"**. That is the
   landmine above, proven not to fire end to end.
3. The confirm for an academic-only wipe read *"This will clear: • what she is
   working on — back to the start of Year 1"* and did **not** mention animals.
4. The red button rendered greyed and `disabled` with nothing ticked; ticking a
   row brightened it and dimmed the other two. No console errors.

Screenshots were taken to the scratchpad and not committed.

*Caveat, stated honestly:* the browser run was against a **fresh** island (0
friends, 1 piece of land), so it exercised the singular wording. Seeding a
lived-in save was attempted and abandoned — the durable store ranks a
hand-written localStorage doc as `rev 0` (`adoptLegacy`) and the IndexedDB
envelope written on first boot outranks it, so the seed never loads. Anyone
wanting a lived-in island in a browser must seal a real envelope with a valid
checksum. The plural counts ("3 friends", "12 pieces of land") are covered by
the DOM tests instead.
