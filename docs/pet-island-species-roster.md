# Pet Island — Species Roster & Collections (handover)

Brainstorm output, ratified in conversation 28 Jul. **~296 new builds
across 20 collections**, on top of the live base 24. Not a build order —
collections ship one at a time, on the existing 85% unlock cadence.

---

## 1. Build rules (standing)

- **Match the live Kenney cube-pet look.** Same proportions, same flat
  palette language, same face-decal soul. A new species must sit beside
  `animal-fox` without looking like a guest.
- **Primitives where possible** — boxes, cones, spheres, cylinders. No
  bespoke sculpting. Fred is the precedent: parts + programmatic colour.
- **Kits before species.** Build the kit once; species become data
  (proportions, palette, two or three detail parts). Kit inventory:
  1. **Quadruped** (~150 species) — body, head, ears, four legs, tail.
  2. **Songbird** (~35) — body, wings, beak, tail fan, legs.
  3. **Raptor** (16) — hooked beak, broad wings, tail fan, talons.
  4. **Swim** (~30) — body, fins, tail; no legs.
  5. **Minibeast** (~16) — segmented body, six legs, optional wings.
  6. **Bespoke** (~30) — octopus, jellyfish, snake, kangaroo, sloth,
     the dinosaurs, the odder legendaries.
- **The live 24 are frozen** (`animal-beaver … animal-tiger`). Never
  rebuilt, never restyled. But their **geometry is fair game as a
  starting point** for new builds: bear from panda, wolf from dog, pony
  from horse, hare from bunny, boar is already `animal-hog`.
- Every species carries its signature behaviour (see the species-draft
  document for the verb list — perch, cling, curl, sentry, pet-to-pet
  landing, dusk-active).

### Conservation collections — design notes
- **Rarity mirrors reality**: the more threatened the collection, the
  rarer the hatch. Critically Endangered is the rarest tier in the game
  after Legendary. The lesson lands wordlessly.
- Statuses are recorded as data with a `checkedDate`, not baked into
  prose, so a future reassessment is an edit, not a rewrite.
- **Threat badge (cross-cutting)**: any species in these four
  collections — *and* any base-24 species that is threatened in reality
  (tiger, elephant, giraffe, lion, koala, polar bear, panda) — carries a
  small status badge in the album wherever it lives. Sensitivity by
  encounter, not by segregation.

## 3. Deterministic names (the social layer)

Each pet keeps two names:

- **Species name** — real, fixed, printed: the playground currency.
  "Have you got the pangolin?" works across every child's island.
- **Given name** — decodable, generated by the existing alien-word
  generator but **seeded deterministically from `species + set`**, so
  every child's blue-set hedgehog is the same Bimo, forever. Shared
  vocabulary and the decodable-reading mechanic, in one object.

Consequences to honour:
- Fixed names cannot adapt to a child's phonics level, so **name
  difficulty rides collection order**: early collections draw short
  CVC-ish names; later collections may draw longer ones.
- Names are generated ONCE, audited (pronunciation + no real-word or
  rude-word collisions), and then **frozen as data** — never
  regenerated, or the shared bond breaks.
- Name audio is baked in the teacher voice (Olivia) as part of the
  normal corpus.

## 5. Album badges (the reading incentive)

Every species gets a short "zoo placard" in the album, revealed on tap.
**Deliberately never read aloud** — the badge is a reason to want to
read. Rules:

- **Two lines per species.** Line 1: a simple, mostly-decodable
  sentence for early readers ("A hedgehog rolls up into a ball."). Line
  2: a richer fact for later ("It can have up to 7,000 spines and sleeps
  all winter."). Both visible; the second rewards growing skill.
- Facts are true, checkable, and cheerful — no predation gore, no
  extinction despair; conservation framed as *people helping*.
- UK English, UK-relevant framing where possible.
- Collection pages get a one-line placard too ("Raptors are birds that
  hunt. They have hooked beaks and huge eyes.").
- Text lives in data (`data/badges.json`), authorable in the workbench
  alongside the lesson editor, and is explicitly **excluded from the
  narration corpus** — no clip is ever baked for a badge.
